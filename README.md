# Tune Up Community Acupuncture

![Tune Up Community Acupuncture Website](https://pub-7fc7af170ed84c0c928d3cd4ecfa1ea5.r2.dev/web-thumbnail/2.thumbnail-tune-up-community-acupuncture.png)

## 🌿 About the Website

**Tune Up Community Acupuncture** is a modern, responsive website for a community-based acupuncture clinic located in Oakland, CA. The website showcases the clinic's services, philosophy, and commitment to making holistic healthcare accessible and affordable for everyone.

### ✨ Features

- 📱 Fully responsive design for all devices
- 🎨 Clean, calming aesthetic with nature-inspired colors
- 📅 Online appointment booking system
- 📝 Contact form with PHP backend
- 🏥 Detailed service pages and case studies
- ⭐ Patient testimonials and success stories
- 📰 Blog section for health tips and news

## 👩‍⚕️ About Dr. Eun Kim, LAc

**Eun Kim** is a Licensed Acupuncturist and the founder of Tune Up Community Acupuncture. She graduated from the **American College of Traditional Chinese Medicine** in San Francisco in 2009.

With over **15 years of experience**, Dr. Kim has worked in private fertility clinics and community clinics including Tiny Needles and Alameda Community Acupuncture before founding Tune Up Community Acupuncture.

### 💚 Philosophy

Dr. Kim believes in the social model of business that prioritizes community over profit. Her mission is to:

> *"Widen access to healthcare by making acupuncture accessible, effective, and affordable—not just for some of us, but for all of us."*

She is dedicated to growing marginalized communities' social and political power through access to health and healing.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Clone the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/stynerdev225/tune-up-community-acupuncture.git
   ```

2. Start a local server:
   ```bash
   php -S localhost:3000 -t website
   ```

3. Open your browser and visit `http://localhost:3000`
