Menstrual Cramp Relief

Presenting Issues

Debilitating cramps and irregular cycles were eased with acupuncture, leading to more predictable and comfortable periods.

Background Information

Assessment

Women's Health treatment focused on easing cramps and regulating menstrual cycles.

Treatment Plan

Goals
  1. Ease debilitating menstrual cramps.
  2. Regulate menstrual cycles for more predictable periods.
Treatment Approach
