Any Questions

What is community acupuncture?

Community acupuncture is treatment in a shared, quiet room setting where multiple people rest at the same time while receiving care. This model allows us to keep costs low and make acupuncture accessible to more people.

How much does treatment cost?

We offer sliding-scale pricing, roughly in the $20-50 range per treatment, to keep care affordable for people with different incomes. Pay what you can afford.

What conditions can acupuncture treat?

We treat chronic pain, stress, anxiety, PTSD, insomnia, depression, digestive disorders, allergies, hypertension, hormonal balance, fertility support, and general wellness based on Traditional Chinese Medicine.

How can I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by calling us at 510-519-8085, emailing info@tuneupca.com, or filling out our online appointment request form.

What should I expect during my first visit?

You'll receive gentle, trauma-informed care in a calm, shared treatment space. Each needle is applied with a caring touch, and you can rest or nap during treatment. We offer face-up acupuncture, back and neck acupuncture, and cupping therapy.

Do you accept insurance?

Our sliding-scale model is designed to be affordable without insurance. Contact us directly to discuss payment options and any special discounts that may be available.
