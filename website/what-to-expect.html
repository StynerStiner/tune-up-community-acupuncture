What to Expect

FAQ

Does acupuncture hurt?

Most people feel little to no pain. The needles are hair-thin, and you may feel a slight pinch or tingling sensation. Many patients find the treatment deeply relaxing.

FAQ

How long does a session last?

Sessions typically last 30-60 minutes. After needle placement, you'll rest comfortably while the needles work their magic. This is your time to relax and let your body heal.

FAQ

How many treatments will I need?

It varies by condition and individual. Acute issues may improve in 3-5 sessions, while chronic conditions often benefit from ongoing care. We'll discuss a treatment plan during your first visit.

FAQ

What should I wear to my appointment?

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that can be easily rolled up to access your arms and legs. Most acupuncture points are below the elbows and knees.

FAQ

Is acupuncture safe?

Yes, when performed by a licensed acupuncturist. We use sterile, single-use needles and follow strict safety protocols. Side effects are rare and typically mild.

FAQ

Will my insurance cover acupuncture?

Many insurance plans now cover acupuncture. We recommend checking with your provider. We can provide receipts for reimbursement if needed.

Your Visit

What to Expect

1

Self Check-In

New Patients

Please have your new patient forms filled out online via JaneApp before you arrive. I will greet you as soon as possible and help you find a table or chair to receive your treatment.

Returning Patients

Come in and choose your favorite table or chair, get comfortable and I will be right over.

2

Choose a Table or Chair

Rest in/on for 30-60 minutes

Pick anywhere you feel most comfortable. Not every table or chair may have a heat lamp available. Consider this when choosing your spot.

Heat lamps are used to address pain or for plain old extra cozy warmth.

3

Get Comfortable

  • Remove your shoes and socks
  • Roll up your sleeves and pant legs
  • Queue up your meditation or music if you want to listen with earbuds quietly during your treatment
  • Please don't use/look through your phone during your treatment
Let us know if any needles are uncomfortable and if you need to leave at a specific time.
Accessibility

Access Information

Ground Level Access

We are on the ground level with no stairs. Our entrance is accessible with 3-4 feet of space between massage tables and reclining chairs to move freely through.

Accessible Bathroom

Our bathroom is fully accessible and equipped with grab bars for your safety and comfort.

Mobility Accommodations

If you use a wheelchair or other mobility device, we have a massage table lowered to 2 feet from the floor for ease of transfer. We can also treat you in your mobility chair/device.

Electric Lift Chairs

Our two reclining chairs are electric lift chairs to help make getting in and out of a seated position more comfortable from a standing position.

Low Scent Space

We are a low-scent space. We lightly diffuse essential oils and sometimes burn incense. We will turn off the diffuser or not burn incense on the day of your treatment, by request.

Accessible Parking

There is one accessible parking space 200 feet from the clinic entrance just outside of 1600 San Pablo Ave.
